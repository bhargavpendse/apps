SMS Shield

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

SMS Shield is an SMS app that sorts your messages, warns you about security alerts and scams, and shows where your money goes. Everything happens on your phone. SMS Shield has no internet access, so it cannot send your messages or any other data anywhere.

What the app uses, and why

PermissionWhy SMS Shield needs it
SMS (read, receive, send)To work as your SMS app: show, sort, search and send messages, and detect scams, phishing and account security alerts. SMS Shield reads SMS only while it is your default SMS app.
ContactsTo show names instead of numbers, and to let you choose a trusted contact.
Phone stateTo know which SIM a message came on, and to warn you if your SIM changes.
NotificationsTo show new messages, security alarms, reminders and the monthly money summary.
Start at bootTo keep your reminders after the phone restarts.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer never receives your messages, contacts, money information or any other data.

  • Messages, categories, reminders and money insights are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • The app has no internet permission, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • SMS Shield Pro (optional) is bought through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment under Google's privacy policy; SMS Shield only asks Google Play on your phone whether Pro has been bought. The developer never receives your payment details.
  • The app's data is excluded from Android cloud backup and from phone-to-phone transfer.
  • Money insights are calculated on your phone from your bank SMS. They are never uploaded.

SMS the app sends

SMS Shield sends an SMS only when you send one, or, if you turn on the trusted-contact feature, to the contact you chose when a serious security alert arrives. Your operator's normal SMS charges apply.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling SMS Shield deletes all of its data from your phone. Your messages remain in the phone's shared SMS storage, where your next SMS app can read them.

Children

SMS Shield is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
